# Quant Plus full product expansion

## Goal
Implement the twelve agreed product improvements and five differentiating features as one cohesive, trustworthy simulated trading system.

## Delivery approach
1. Audit and extend the existing foundation without replacing working wallet, order, auth, or market flows.
2. Add secure account-scoped data for advanced orders, alerts, journals, saved layouts, preferences, and risk limits.
3. Build an integrated instrument workspace with professional charting, trading, context, and persisted layout choices.
4. Add advanced order review, consequence previews, portfolio/risk intelligence, replay, alerts, and journaling.
5. Upgrade Quant AI to one evidence-led answer and connect it contextually across the product.
6. Harden all states, keyboard use, reduced motion, responsiveness, and performance.
7. Validate all routes and critical end-to-end workflows at desktop and mobile sizes.

## Product principles
- Never imply that demonstrative market data or AI output is live.
- Never move real money or securities; all trading remains simulated.
- Keep a single definitive Quant AI verdict with evidence, risks, invalidation, confidence, and timestamp.
- Preserve existing wallet settlement, holdings, and private account isolation.
- Prefer dense, calm, professional interfaces over decorative complexity.

## Technical scope
- TanStack Start routes and existing shared app shell remain authoritative.
- Database additions require explicit grants, RLS, authenticated-only RPC access, and user-scoped policies.
- Advanced order state transitions remain atomic on the server.
- Motion remains transform/opacity based and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Route-specific metadata remains complete for every new page.
