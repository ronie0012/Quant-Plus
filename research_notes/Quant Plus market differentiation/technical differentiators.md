# Quant Plus technical differentiators

## 1. What is actually implemented?

### Takeaway

The checkout is best described as a **local-first, evidence-aware NSE cash-equity research and paper-trading terminal**. Its most defensible technical distinction is the implemented connection between decision evidence, server-authoritative paper execution, explicit risk controls, provenance, and post-trade review; this is a product combination, not a claim that any single component is unprecedented. [`README.md:1-17`](../../README.md) and the repository feature inventory describe the same bounded scope, including virtual NSE delivery equity, a separate research system, a read-only copilot, and explicit exclusions. [`feature inventory.md:217-219`](feature%20inventory.md)

### Cited Findings

#### Paper execution integrity

- **The browser does not choose the execution price.** The paper route resolves the authenticated book service, fetches an execution-eligible provider quote on the server, and passes that observation into the shared paper-book command. A persisted intent is replayed before a new quote is fetched. [`src/routes/api/paper/$.ts:175-191`](../../src/routes/api/paper/$.ts) The shared quote policy requires a positive finite price, matching symbol, Fyers source, valid timestamp, non-delayed/non-stale data, an open verified session for live mode, and an explicit separate mode for a bounded last-session practice snapshot. [`src/lib/quote-eligibility.ts:15-52`](../../src/lib/quote-eligibility.ts)
- **Fills preserve an observation record rather than only a fill price.** `QuoteObservation` includes symbol, price, bid/ask, displayed sizes, provider, provider time, receipt time, decision time, session, delay/stale flags, and change percentage; fills retain reference price, method, liquidity limitation, slippage, charges, and the quote observation. [`src/lib/paper-book/types.ts:10-26`](../../src/lib/paper-book/types.ts) [`src/lib/paper-book/types.ts:63-80`](../../src/lib/paper-book/types.ts)
- **The fill model distinguishes market evidence from a generic midpoint.** A valid spread uses the ask for a buy and bid for a sell, with displayed top-of-book size; an unusable spread falls back to last trade plus the configured slippage assumption. The engine can produce partial fills when displayed liquidity is insufficient, and market-order remainder is cancelled rather than silently filled at an invented quantity. [`src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts:314-329`](../../src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts) [`src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts:437-563`](../../src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts)
- **The paper book has a state machine and audit surface.** It supports open, partial, filled, cancelled, expired and rejected states; reservations; versioned amendments; DAY expiry; protections; itemised charges; append-only ledger/events; and per-order audit data. [`src/lib/paper-book/types.ts:4-7`](../../src/lib/paper-book/types.ts) [`src/lib/paper-book/commands.ts:466-499`](../../src/lib/paper-book/commands.ts) [`IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md:130-160`](../../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md)
- **Retries are designed as intent replays rather than new orders.** The command layer fingerprints order parameters, stores the response for an intent ID, returns the original response for a matching retry, and rejects a changed request with the same intent ID. [`src/lib/paper-book/commands.ts:48-90`](../../src/lib/paper-book/commands.ts) The integrity regression covers repeated intents, distinct intentional orders, and changed-parameter conflicts. [`scripts/test-integrity.mjs:74-90`](../../scripts/test-integrity.mjs)
- **The paper worker continues server-side.** A five-second worker obtains a lease, fetches required symbols, marks holdings, expires DAY orders, fills resting orders, evaluates persisted protections and alerts, and uses savepoints around individual operations. The browser monitor is therefore a display/polling surface rather than the sole protection executor. [`src/lib/paper-worker.server.ts:1-7`](../../src/lib/paper-worker.server.ts) [`src/lib/paper-worker.server.ts:160-237`](../../src/lib/paper-worker.server.ts)
- **NSE-specific timing and a conservative settlement model are represented in code.** The exchange calendar records source references and hashes, fails closed for an unverified special session/year, and computes session-close expiry. Settlement records a calendar version, calculates next-clearing-day availability, separates settled/unsettled quantities and cash, and refuses withdrawals against pending or unverified proceeds. [`src/lib/exchange-calendar.ts:1-6`](../../src/lib/exchange-calendar.ts) [`src/lib/exchange-calendar.ts:26-70`](../../src/lib/exchange-calendar.ts) [`src/lib/paper-book/settlement.ts:1-12`](../../src/lib/paper-book/settlement.ts) [`src/lib/paper-book/settlement.ts:59-110`](../../src/lib/paper-book/settlement.ts)
- **Money and fees are explicit rather than display-only.** The engine uses integer paise, debits charges to the ledger, and preserves a dated schedule separating statutory, exchange, regulator and broker-assumption categories. Unknown charge dates fail closed. [`src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts:1-5`](../../src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts) [`src/lib/paper-fees.ts:1-7`](../../src/lib/paper-fees.ts) [`src/lib/paper-fees.ts:64-134`](../../src/lib/paper-fees.ts)

#### Risk controls and decision discipline

- **Server exposure checks are distinct from a UI warning.** New exposure is checked against pause state, marked day loss, max order value, equity and max-position percentage; risk-reducing sells still require shares but do not get blocked by new-exposure limits. [`src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts:286-310`](../../src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts) The integrity suite exercises daily-loss enforcement, deposits that cannot conceal a loss, risk-reducing exits during a pause, and unchanged state after rejection. [`scripts/test-integrity.mjs:92-120`](../../scripts/test-integrity.mjs)
- **Pre-trade review compiles execution, portfolio and scenario risk into a structured preview.** It can use the observed touch, spread, displayed size, ADV, volatility, gap history, beta, fees, concentration, events, behaviour warnings, planned loss, gap loss and daily budget. Unknown inputs become `null` or an explicit note rather than an unlabelled synthetic value. [`src/lib/quant/risk-compiler.ts:1-9`](../../src/lib/quant/risk-compiler.ts) [`src/lib/quant/risk-compiler.ts:135-206`](../../src/lib/quant/risk-compiler.ts) [`src/lib/quant/risk-compiler.ts:235-376`](../../src/lib/quant/risk-compiler.ts)
- **Decision plans freeze the evidence context that led to an order.** A plan stores evidence kind, source, publication time, availability time, counter-thesis, invalidation, levels, sizing logic, expected costs, maximum acceptable loss, market snapshot and risk preview; server-side revision handling prevents later rewriting of the market snapshot/risk preview and preserves plan changes. [`src/lib/quant/decision-plan.ts:1-10`](../../src/lib/quant/decision-plan.ts) [`src/lib/quant/decision-plan.ts:12-54`](../../src/lib/quant/decision-plan.ts) [`src/lib/paper-book/commands.ts:432-464`](../../src/lib/paper-book/commands.ts)
- **Outcome review separates thesis, execution and process.** The decision-plan code can classify a result such as good thesis/bad execution or good trade/bad process, rather than treating P&L as the only grade. The test suite exercises both kinds of separation. [`src/lib/quant/decision-plan.ts:300-341`](../../src/lib/quant/decision-plan.ts) [`src/lib/quant/decision-plan.ts:483-557`](../../src/lib/quant/decision-plan.ts) [`src/lib/quant/quant.test.ts:127-249`](../../src/lib/quant/quant.test.ts)
- **Behavioural controls are consent-based and exit-safe.** The behavioural engine detects patterns such as increasing size after losses, can apply cooling-off/daily-decision/drawdown-sizing/thesis protections, and the tests verify that protections can block new buys without blocking exits. [`feature inventory.md:119-123`](feature%20inventory.md) [`src/lib/quant/quant.test.ts:338-386`](../../src/lib/quant/quant.test.ts)

#### Evidence, grounding and data provenance

- **Quant AI uses bounded typed read tools.** Its evidence builder reads the paper account, holdings, risk window, recent orders, order audit, journal, market context, quote, validated history facts, news tone and saved research; each evidence item receives an ID, kind, source and as-of time. The tools do not place, amend or cancel orders. [`src/lib/copilot/context.server.ts:1-5`](../../src/lib/copilot/context.server.ts) [`src/lib/copilot/context.server.ts:66-107`](../../src/lib/copilot/context.server.ts) [`src/lib/copilot/context.server.ts:192-262`](../../src/lib/copilot/context.server.ts)
- **The copilot has explicit authority and uncertainty boundaries.** The server prompt says it is read-only, cannot tell the user to execute a trade, must distinguish live/closed/delayed/stale data, and must treat opinions, tones and indices as non-probabilities. [`src/routes/api/chat.ts:9-20`](../../src/routes/api/chat.ts)
- **Current code buffers and checks the model answer before emitting it.** It checks numeric claims against cited typed evidence with field, sign, unit, entity and citation binding; rejected unsupported content is omitted and replaced with an evidence-check message. The tests cover invented figures, unknown citations, reversed signs, wrong fields, wrong instruments and invented small quantities. - **Research-agent claims have a separate field/cutoff validation boundary.** The Python research grounding module checks signal fields, numeric signs, percentages, evidence references and evidence after the decision cutoff, and returns an explicit verified/unsupported status. - **News is treated as versioned evidence, not only a current headline list.** The SQLite archive stores content hashes, provider/story identity, versions, supersession, raw payload and first-observation time; append-only triggers prevent rewriting/deleting history. #### Local/InsForge architecture, auth, privacy and persistence

- **The default runtime is local and single-profile.** The application documents `.local-data/workspace.json` as the current store for wallet, orders, holdings, watchlists, alerts, journal and preferences; the local token is a loopback/same-origin boundary marker, not multi-user authentication. [`README.md:27-36`](../../README.md) [`src/lib/workspace-session.ts:1-26`](../../src/lib/workspace-session.ts)
- **Local persistence has stronger integrity mechanics than a casual browser-only demo.** The store validates the v2 book, uses an interprocess writer lock, recovers only stale locks from exited same-host processes, writes through a temporary file with `fsync`, preserves malformed files instead of resetting them, and supports hashed migration backups. [`src/lib/local-store.server.ts:57-131`](../../src/lib/local-store.server.ts) [`src/lib/local-store.server.ts:134-220`](../../src/lib/local-store.server.ts)
- **InsForge is implemented as an explicit hosted provider, not silently mixed with local state.** The hosted adapter runs the same engine/commands, verifies the bearer token to obtain the user ID, loads/reconciles the book with a server-only admin key, commits through compare-and-swap, retries version conflicts, and refuses practice mode in hosted mode. [`src/lib/insforge-book.server.ts:1-7`](../../src/lib/insforge-book.server.ts) [`src/lib/insforge-book.server.ts:49-72`](../../src/lib/insforge-book.server.ts) [`src/lib/insforge-book.server.ts:184-228`](../../src/lib/insforge-book.server.ts) [`src/lib/insforge-book.server.ts:270-290`](../../src/lib/insforge-book.server.ts)
- **The hosted schema encodes ownership and a single write authority.** Paper rows reference `auth.users`, money is bigint paise, intent IDs and sequences are unique per user, ledger/fill/event history is append-only, the commit RPC uses compare-and-swap and exact ledger reconciliation, and row-level security scopes reads to the authenticated owner. [`migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql:1-8`](../../migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql) [`migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql:101-119`](../../migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql) [`migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql:151-224`](../../migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql) [`migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql:322-354`](../../migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql)

## 2. High-level comparison with mainstream product categories

### Takeaway

Mainstream products already cover many individual pieces: charting, screeners, alerts, virtual cash, order tickets, options practice, portfolios, reports, and web-grounded AI answers. The defensible comparison is therefore about **integration and implementation emphasis**, not feature ownership: Quant Plus concentrates on an auditable NSE paper-account lifecycle and decision-review loop, while public materials for common products emphasize broad market access, simulation, analytics, reporting, or general web search. The repository’s own comparison is explicitly a dated desk-research comparison, not hands-on benchmarking of competitor accuracy, latency or accessibility. [`audit/implementation/competitive-comparison-20261014.md:1-7`](../../audit/implementation/competitive-comparison-20261014.md)

## 3. Defensible wording, evidence limits and claim ladder

### Takeaway

Use implementation-specific language that says what the repository does and where its boundaries are. The strongest wording is “unusual combination,” “auditable paper-book design,” “server-authoritative paper execution,” “typed/read-only evidence grounding,” and “local-first with an opt-in InsForge path.” Avoid “unique,” “first,” “market-leading,” “institutional-grade,” “realistic in all market conditions,” “validated predictive edge,” and “fully private multi-user SaaS” unless new independent evidence is added.
