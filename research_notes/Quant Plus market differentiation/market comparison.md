## What the public comparison suggests for differentiation

1. **Evidence-grounded AI is a distinct product layer.** Quartr and Fiscal.ai publicly describe source-linked or auditable first-party financial research. The Indian retail products reviewed publicly describe data tools, strategy builders, assistants, or algo workflows, but none of the cited pages establishes an equivalent claim-level source trail. This is an opportunity for Quant-Plus only if its evidence links remain visible, time-stamped, and verifiable in the user workflow.
2. **Paper trading and review are split across products.** TradingView has the most extensive simulator detail in the reviewed docs; Neostox offers an explicitly practice-first Indian workflow with performance reports but states that prices are delayed by 30 days; Zerodha Console offers the most explicit live-account P&L and tax/accounting documentation in this set, while a general paper workflow is unverified. Quant-Plus can position around decision → controlled paper execution → outcome attribution, while being clear that its current book is NSE cash-equity only.
3. **Options are a mature comparison point.** Sensibull and Upstox publicly document multi-leg construction, Greeks/OI/IV, basket or bulk execution, and target/stop-loss workflows; Neostox also covers virtual options. Quant-Plus should treat options as a future capability gate, not imply parity in the current product.
4. **Portfolio accounting is not the same as decision memory.** Zerodha Console is the most explicitly documented in this set for broker-linked true P&L, charges, corporate actions, tax reports, and family consolidation. TradingView is strong in generic portfolio analytics. The more differentiated Quant-Plus layer is the immutable decision-time snapshot, evidence/thesis/invalidation, and process-quality attribution—but it is currently backed by a paper book rather than broker settlement.
5. **Collaboration needs precise language.** Public social/community sharing exists in TradingView and Sensibull, and Zerodha verifies family portfolio consolidation. That does not establish private team research, shared journals, reviewer permissions, or approval workflows; these remain unknown/unverified for most competitors and gated in the current Quant-Plus checkout.

## Explicitly unverified items to keep out of claims

- Do not describe Kite’s public “Try demo” link as a fully documented paper-trading system without validating its mechanics.
- Do not describe Sensibull’s Strategy Wizard, Neostox’s “AI Options Assistant,” or Upstox Algoverse as evidence-grounded generative AI; the cited public descriptions do not establish source-linked AI reasoning.
- Do not claim that TradingView, Zerodha, Sensibull, Neostox, or Upstox provide private multi-user research/journal collaboration based only on public community, family, or broker-integration features.
- Do not infer Indian exchange/options coverage for Quartr or Fiscal.ai from their global research claims.
- Do not infer absence of a capability from this review. “Unknown / unverified” is the required label where official public evidence was insufficient.
