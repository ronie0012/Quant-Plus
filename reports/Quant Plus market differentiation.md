# Quant Plus Connects Evidence to Practice

Quant-Plus is currently best understood as a **local-first, evidence-aware NSE cash-equity research and paper-practice terminal**. Its shipped value is not a proven stock-picking edge or a broker replacement; it helps a user observe sourced market context, investigate a thesis, define risk, practise the decision in a server-authoritative paper book, and review what was believed against what actually happened. The strongest defensible distinction is an **unusual combination** of research provenance, bounded AI, explicit risk controls, auditable paper accounting, and post-decision attribution—not a claim that any one of those components is globally unique. The local workspace is still the current authority, while InsForge authentication and hosted-book infrastructure exist behind an unapproved cutover. Compared with the market, Quant-Plus is deeper in workflow continuity and execution evidence but behind on instruments, mobile reach, broker connectivity, collaboration, distribution, and validated outcome quality. The product owner should therefore position it around **disciplined, inspectable decision practice for Indian equities**, with every stronger claim tied to a specific implementation or completed evidence gate.

**Inference from the implemented sequence.** The product’s value is cumulative: each stage leaves evidence for the next stage. A stock page can carry into a Decision Canvas; a plan can link to an order; the order stores the quote, fill method, charges, and events; Review Hub then brings the plan, journal, outcome, and execution record back together. That continuity is more meaningful than a long list of isolated pages because it gives the user one answer to four questions: *What did I know? What did I believe? What did the simulator do? ## The paper book makes evidence and risk operational

**Paper execution is stronger than a balance-update demo.** The browser does not supply the authoritative fill price. The server resolves the book service, fetches an execution-eligible provider quote, validates symbol, source, timestamp, session, freshness, and required fields, and passes the observation into the paper-book command. A valid spread uses the ask for a buy and bid for a sell; displayed top-of-book size can limit the fill; an unusable spread falls back to last trade plus an explicit slippage assumption. The book records open, partial, filled, cancelled, expired, and rejected states, reservations, versioned amendments, DAY expiry, itemised charges, append-only ledger/events, and per-order audit data. ([`src/routes/api/paper/$.ts:175-191`](../src/routes/api/paper/$.ts), [`src/lib/quote-eligibility.ts:15-52`](../src/lib/quote-eligibility.ts), [`src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts:314-329`](../src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts), [`IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md:130-160`](../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md))

The server worker continues to expire orders, mark holdings, fill resting orders, evaluate protections and alerts, and preserve a lease even when the browser is closed. Idempotent intent fingerprints mean a retry replays the original order response rather than silently creating a duplicate; changed parameters under the same intent are rejected. Integer-paise accounting and a dated, itemised fee schedule make the simulator financially explicit, while settlement-aware balances distinguish what is available from what is pending. These are concrete implementation properties, not proof of exchange-level realism: queue priority, broker-specific behavior, corporate actions, a verified broker fee profile, and complete clearing integration remain outside the delivered model. ([`src/lib/paper-worker.server.ts:1-237`](../src/lib/paper-worker.server.ts), [`src/lib/paper-book/commands.ts:48-90`](../src/lib/paper-book/commands.ts), [`src/lib/paper-fees.ts:64-134`](../src/lib/paper-fees.ts), [`PROJECT_PRESENTATION.md:578-600`](../PROJECT_PRESENTATION.md))

**Risk is both previewed and enforced.** Before an order, the compiler can show planned loss, gap loss, fees, slippage, liquidity, sector exposure, daily budget usage, and warnings. Server-side exposure checks enforce pause state, marked day loss, maximum order value, equity, and maximum position percentage; risk-reducing sells still require shares but are not blocked by new-exposure limits. Persisted stop, target, and trailing rules generate deterministic exit intents through the worker, and behavioural protections can require a thesis, cooling-off period, daily decision limit, drawdown sizing, or an explanation of why a new entry differs. This is a credible discipline layer for paper practice; it should not be presented as a guarantee against all market, provider, or model failure. ([`src/lib/quant/risk-compiler.ts:135-206`](../src/lib/quant/risk-compiler.ts), [`src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts:286-310`](../src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts), [`src/lib/paper-worker.server.ts:160-325`](../src/lib/paper-worker.server.ts), [`src/components/app/pages/BehaviorPage.tsx:113-255`](../src/components/app/pages/BehaviorPage.tsx))

**Data handling is deliberately explicit about missingness.** Market pages show provider, provider time, live/closed/delayed/stale state, coverage, and unavailable conditions. Invalid OHLCV bars are dropped and counted rather than repaired, indicators remain null during warm-up, and the news archive preserves content hashes, story versions, supersession, first observation, raw payload, and append-only history. Typed event extraction retains amounts, percentages, periods, directions, comparison basis, certainty, and supporting text spans; conflicting or unsupported directions abstain. Yahoo can support labelled research/display history, but it cannot turn a delayed fallback into execution evidence. ([`src/components/app/stocks/StockDetail.tsx:60-211`](../src/components/app/stocks/StockDetail.tsx), [`src/lib/market-history.ts:22-55`](../src/lib/market-history.ts), [`backend-extension/quantplus_intelligence/news/archive.py:32-60`](../backend-extension/quantplus_intelligence/news/archive.py), [`backend-extension/quantplus_intelligence/news/events.py:35-75`](../backend-extension/quantplus_intelligence/news/events.py), [`src/lib/quote-eligibility.ts:27-52`](../src/lib/quote-eligibility.ts))

**AI is bounded by account authority and evidence checks.** Quant AI is a read-only copilot: it can inspect typed evidence from the paper account, holdings, risk window, orders and audits, journal, market context, quote, deterministic history facts, news, and saved research, but it cannot place, amend, or cancel an order. The current route buffers the answer and checks numeric claims against evidence IDs for field, sign, unit, entity, and citation binding; unsupported content can be withheld or marked in an evidence check. The stock-research system is separate: its specialist graph produces research-only sessions and labels conviction as an uncalibrated opinion. The honest claim is therefore **typed, time-aware, read-only evidence grounding with automated claim checks**, not “hallucination-proof,” “accurate,” or “predictive” AI. ## Local mode is current; InsForge is a gated hosted path

**Current runtime: local and single-profile.** The default application stores wallet, orders, holdings, watchlists, alerts, journal, and preferences in `.local-data/workspace.json`. The local store validates the book, uses a writer lock, writes through a temporary file with `fsync`, preserves malformed files instead of silently resetting them, and supports backups and migration tooling. The local token is a loopback/same-origin boundary marker; it is not multi-user authentication. Offline snapshots are read-only, and mutating workspace or paper calls refuse to run until the connection returns. ([`README.md:27-36`](../README.md), [`src/lib/local-store.server.ts:57-220`](../src/lib/local-store.server.ts), [`src/lib/workspace-session.ts:1-26`](../src/lib/workspace-session.ts), [`src/lib/workspace-api.ts:10-30`](../src/lib/workspace-api.ts))

**InsForge implementation: present but not the default service.** The hosted path includes InsForge auth, HttpOnly refresh cookies, owner-scoped row-level security, an admin-only `paper_commit_book` compare-and-swap write authority, an imported and reconciled book, pagination, history reconciliation, and fail-closed behavior when only part of the hosted configuration is present. The repository also records a dated hosted isolation run covering owner isolation, browser-ledger-write rejection, CAS conflicts, and reconciliation behavior. This is evidence that the hosted infrastructure has been built and tested at checkpoints; it is not evidence that the public application has completed cutover. ([`src/lib/insforge-book.server.ts:49-72`](../src/lib/insforge-book.server.ts), [`migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql:101-119`](../migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql), [`migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql:151-224`](../migrations/20261014130103_create-paper-book.sql), [`audit/implementation/insforge/live-isolation-20261014.md:1-18`](../audit/implementation/insforge/live-isolation-20261014.md))

The operational distinction matters. The local book remains active while hosted cutover validation is pending; activation requires both `QUANT_PLUS_BOOK_PROVIDER=insforge` and `VITE_QUANT_PLUS_AUTH=insforge`. Last-session practice is local-only and hidden for hosted users, and hosted mode cannot silently fall back to the loopback profile. Hosted multi-user collaboration—shared workspaces, shared watchlists and journals, roles, review permissions, and public links—is not delivered merely because hosted persistence exists. The accurate product language is **“local-first with an opt-in InsForge hosted path”**, not “fully deployed hosted SaaS” or “multi-user collaboration.” ([`README.md:11-17`](../README.md), [`INSFORGE_MIGRATION.md:16-19`](../INSFORGE_MIGRATION.md), [`INSFORGE_MIGRATION.md:103-126`](../INSFORGE_MIGRATION.md), [`src/components/app/pages/ResearchNetworkPage.tsx:536-617`](../src/components/app/pages/ResearchNetworkPage.tsx))

**What is genuinely distinctive.** The research supports three careful positioning inferences. First, **inspectable research plus a hard deterministic authority boundary** is a meaningful combination: specialist outputs and opposing cases can be examined, but deterministic code owns account mutation and risk enforcement. Third, **decision lifecycle plus outcome attribution** can distinguish thesis quality, execution quality, and process quality rather than grading every trade only by P&L. ## Demo the complete decision lifecycle

The strongest product-owner demo should follow **one NSE equity from observation to review**, rather than tour every page or show an isolated AI answer. The sequence below makes the proposed differentiation visible and keeps the boundaries honest.

1. **Observe — show what is knowable.** Open one supported NSE equity. ## Conclusion

Quant-Plus should compete on **continuity and trust across a narrow decision lifecycle**, not on breadth or predictive bravado. The product already has enough implementation depth to make a compelling case that a paper trade can be tied to an evidence context, risk preview, server-controlled account transition, durable audit trail, and later process review. That is a credible workflow proposition for serious Indian-equity learners even while options, live routing, mobile, collaboration, and hosted cutover remain outside the current release.

The next strategic proof is not another isolated feature. It is repeated, observable completion of the loop with reliable provider coverage, faithful paper accounting, reviewed AI usefulness, matured outcome evidence, and a clean hosted transition. If those gates pass, Quant-Plus can strengthen its position from an unusual implementation combination into a demonstrated learning and decision-quality product. Until then, the strongest public wording remains: **“Quant Plus is an evidence-aware research and paper-practice workspace for Indian equities that connects sourced context and inspectable AI analysis to deterministic risk checks, auditable paper execution, and post-decision review—without claiming predictive edge or live brokerage.”**
