# Turn Every Trading Decision Into Evidence

Quant-Plus should build exactly one flagship concept: **Decision Flight Recorder**. It is a sealed, replayable record of one paper decision that preserves what the user could know, what they believed, what they risked, what the paper book did, and how the same thesis behaved on a hidden historical path. Its two supporting parts are the **Evidence Ledger**, which freezes dated source and market context, and the **Thesis Flight Simulator**, which rehearses the locked plan without look-ahead. The WOW is not an AI announcing a winner; it is the moment a user moves from a live-looking decision to a blind historical reveal and then back to an auditable explanation of the result. Quant-Plus is unusually ready because the code already contains structured decision plans, evidence claims, paper-book accounting, replay mechanics, risk controls, and outcome attribution. The near-term product decision is therefore to make those foundations feel like one product, locally first and paper-only, while treating predictive quality, broker execution, options, hosted SaaS, and collaboration as unshipped or conditional. The defensible positioning is not “undefeatable AI”; it is a permissioned longitudinal record of decision quality that becomes harder to replace every time a user creates and reviews another flight.

## The missing product object is the decision, not another feature

A trader’s real problem is not a shortage of charts, screeners, alerts, or opinions. It is that the chain from idea to learning is scattered: a chart lives in one tool, a filing in another, a note in a spreadsheet, a simulated fill in a paper account, and the explanation for the trade disappears once the outcome is known. Decision Flight Recorder makes the decision itself the product object. A user starts with “I am considering a paper position in an NSE equity before its result,” and Quant-Plus turns that intent into a structured contract: evidence, thesis, counter-thesis, invalidation, horizon, entry, stop, target, time exit, size, maximum loss, costs, and either a trade or a deliberate no-trade. The user then locks it. Later, the recorder can answer four questions with one identity: **What did I know? What did I believe? What did I risk? What should I learn?**

The subcomponents have precise jobs. The Evidence Ledger is not a generic document repository or chatbot citation footer. It is a point-in-time ledger of claims with source, publication time, availability time, market observation, calculation, model version, contradiction, decision, and outcome links. The existing `EvidenceClaim` type and `WhyButton` already point in this direction ([`evidence.ts`](../src/lib/quant/evidence.ts)). The Thesis Flight Simulator is not a second paper account and not a price oracle. It is a controlled what-if environment that reveals bars and news only as they become available, accepts the same thesis and rules, models stated spread/latency/participation/charges assumptions, and records partial fills, gaps, halts, stop events, thesis revisions, and counterfactuals. The current replay engine already hides future bars and news and keeps a separate replay wallet ([`flight-sim.ts`](../src/lib/quant/flight-sim.ts)). The Decision Flight Recorder is the immutable wrapper that joins those artifacts to the actual paper outcome and later attribution.

This creates a genuine WOW moment because it makes uncertainty personal and visible. The user sees a bullish thesis, a counter-case, and a proposed size that breaches an event or sector boundary. They choose “wait” or “enter paper,” then face a historical path without knowing whether the next event is a gap, flat reaction, delayed confirmation, or stop-through gap. The reveal does not say “the AI knew.” It shows what the original rule would have done, which evidence was available, and how waiting or smaller size changed the result under declared assumptions.

The market evidence supports choosing this integrated workflow over another horizontal surface. TradingView already combines charts, screeners, alerts, paper trading, replay, options, brokers, mobile, and community ([TradingView Features](https://www.tradingview.com/features/), accessed 18 Oct 2026). Neostox markets Indian virtual stocks, futures, options, replay, reports, and Analyze → Trade → Review, while stating that prices are delayed by 30 days ([Neostox](https://neostox.com/), accessed 18 Oct 2026). Sensibull is stronger for options strategy construction and broker-linked workflows ([Sensibull](https://sensibull.com/), accessed 18 Oct 2026). Quartr and Fiscal.ai show that source-linked financial research is already a product category ([Quartr AI Chat](https://quartr.com/features/ai-chat), accessed 18 Oct 2026; [Fiscal.ai Terminal](https://fiscal.ai/products/terminal/), accessed 18 Oct 2026). Quant-Plus should not claim ownership of any of these primitives in isolation.

Generic AI signals and agents are weaker bets for the same reason. Public describes agents that clarify missing parameters, assemble workflows, request approval, monitor markets, and can execute across instruments; Robinhood describes agents that scan, analyze, build watchlists, rebalance, and trade through dedicated controls ([Public prompting guide](https://public.com/ai-agents/how-it-works), 19 Sep 2026; [Public AI Agents](https://public.com/ai-agents), accessed 18 Oct 2026; [Robinhood Agents](https://robinhood.com/us/en/agents/), accessed 18 Oct 2026). FINRA’s January 27, 2026 observations specifically identify autonomy, authority, auditability, data sensitivity, hallucination, bias, and reward misalignment as agent risks ([FINRA AI-agent observations](https://www.finra.org/media-center/blog/observations-on-ai-agents), 27 Jan 2026). A fluent agent shell can be copied or attached to a broker. A decision record that preserves pre-outcome evidence, user intent, execution assumptions, corrections, and review quality is materially harder to recreate.

The concept fits because the difficult primitives are already shipped. `DecisionPlanBody` captures snapshot, evidence, thesis, counter-thesis, invalidation, levels, sizing, costs, maximum loss, trade/no-trade, and revisions ([`decision-plan.ts`](../src/lib/quant/decision-plan.ts)). The paper book records quote observations, fill method, liquidity, charges, reservations, protections, ledger/events, settlement, and idempotent intents ([`types.ts`](../src/lib/paper-book/types.ts); [`engine.ts`](../src/lib/paper-book/engine.ts); [`commands.ts`](../src/lib/paper-book/commands.ts)). `attributeOutcome()` separates thesis, execution, process, and P&L. Decision Canvas, blind replay, and Review Hub already provide the user-facing seams ([`DecisionCanvas.tsx`](../src/components/app/stocks/DecisionCanvas.tsx); [`FlightSimulatorPage.tsx`](../src/components/app/pages/FlightSimulatorPage.tsx); [`ReviewHub.tsx`](../src/components/app/pages/ReviewHub.tsx)). The flagship is an integration decision, not a new unrelated subsystem.

The current boundaries strengthen the wedge. Quant-Plus is local-first with `.local-data/workspace.json` active; InsForge auth/book infrastructure exists behind cutover checks, but this is not completed hosted SaaS ([`README.md`](../README.md); [`INSFORGE_MIGRATION.md`](../INSFORGE_MIGRATION.md)). Paper scope is virtual NSE cash-equity delivery; options, futures, BSE/global tradeable instruments, live routing, and corporate-action-adjusted accounting are unavailable. Research conviction is uncalibrated, 11 configurations did not beat the best constant baseline, and the copilot’s human-reviewed gate remains open ([`IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`](../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md)). The flagship should use these limits as design constraints.

## The two-minute demo should reveal three timelines

The primary user is a serious self-directed Indian-equity learner who already has market tools but loses the reasoning behind each decision. They need to know whether they were wrong about the thesis, early on timing, careless about size, poorly filled, or exposed to an unresolved path. Decision Flight Recorder makes that learning job immediate for one supported NSE equity and lets no-trade count as a completed decision.

| Time | Demo action | The visible WOW and honest boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:20** | Open a supported NSE equity from Discover. Show provider, as-of time, session/freshness, chart, available news/events, and any missing fields. Click **Create decision flight** in Decision Canvas. | The product starts with what was knowable, not an invented quote. The flow is conditional on provider coverage and remains paper-only. |
| **0:20–0:45** | Enter: “I expect margin improvement to support a two-week paper position before the result.” Answer the minimum prompts for metric definition, counter-thesis, invalidation, quantity or risk budget, and no-trade condition. | The idea becomes a compact decision contract. The system can identify a missing assumption, but it does not turn model confidence into a probability or a buy command. |
| **0:45–1:05** | Open the Evidence Ledger. Show the result/announcement, prior guidance, market observation, one supporting claim, one weakening claim, publication/availability times, contradictions, and the packet hash. Click a claim to open the source and its “available before decision?” result. | The user sees the evidence graph rather than a polished summary. Unknown or conflicting timing remains visible. |
| **1:05–1:25** | Click **Lock & rehearse**. Choose **enter paper**, **wait**, **reduce size**, or **no trade**. If entering, submit separately through the existing paper order boundary; if not, record the reason. | The original packet cannot be silently rewritten. A replay action cannot mutate cash, holdings, orders, fills, or the main ledger. |
| **1:25–1:50** | Run a blind historical flight using the same symbol, thesis, levels, quantity, and stated assumptions. Step through bars and news; choose an action before future information is revealed. | The simulator shows a hidden path, a gap or partial fill, and the information available at each cursor. It is a rule-based rehearsal, not exchange-grade microstructure or a prediction. |
| **1:50–2:00** | Reveal the three timelines: **Knowable then** (locked ledger), **What happened** (paper quote/fill/charge/protection/outcome), and **What-if** (blind replay, counterfactual wait/half-size/no-trade, drawdown, and unresolved markers). End at Review Hub with “good thesis/bad execution,” “bad thesis/good execution,” or unresolved. | The same decision identity survives from evidence to practice to learning. The result is inspectable even when the correct answer is “we cannot resolve this from daily data.” |

The demo should reveal a missing metric, conflicting statement, or risk-boundary violation—not a stock pick. That is impressive without claiming alpha. Alpaca’s documentation is a useful honesty model because it lists simulation exclusions such as market impact, latency slippage, queue position, regulatory fees, and dividends ([Alpaca Paper Trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/docs/paper-trading), updated 7 Jul 2026). Quant-Plus should put its own exclusions beside every replay summary.

## Build the seam in 90 days, locally first

The MVP is one vertical slice, not a new AI layer: one supported NSE cash-equity plan, minimum Evidence Ledger packet, locked hash, daily-bar/news replay manifest, blind rehearsal, optional paper-order link, three-timeline result, reflection, and a first-class **NO_TRADE** branch. It is complete only when reload, restart, provider missingness, duplicate actions, stale cursors, and paper-book isolation work.

The first implementation should reuse the current local `research_items` mechanism with `BUNDLE` for the immutable packet and `REPLAY_RESULT` for the projection, while appending `DECISION_FLIGHT_*` events to the existing book event stream. Do not create a second cash or position ledger. The proposed typed object is:

```text
DecisionFlight
  id, version, status, created_at, updated_at
  plan_id, journal_id, order_id | null, symbol, side, decision
  decision_packet_hash
  evidence_bundle, market_snapshot, risk_preview, trust_snapshot
  replay_manifest { provider, interval, bars_as_of,
                    news_archive_as_of, source_fingerprints,
                    start_cursor, hidden_until_cursor }
  scenario { quantity, entry, stop, target, time_exit,
             latency_bars, participation, starting_cash_paise }
  replay_events, replay_fills, markers, summary
  live_outcome | null, counterfactuals | null
  evaluation { coverage, knowability, unresolved_reasons, evaluator_version }
```

The first API can be small and explicit: `POST /api/decision-flights/prepare` validates the existing plan, resolves evidence/history/news, checks eligibility, hashes the packet, and returns the flight ID; `GET /api/decision-flights/:id` returns the read model; `POST /api/decision-flights/:id/action` records a blind action with an idempotent action ID; `POST /api/decision-flights/:id/step` advances only the replay cursor; `POST /api/decision-flights/:id/finalize` writes the summary and review projection; and `GET /api/decision-flights/:id/export` exports the packet, manifest, source hashes, events, and outcome. The paper branch must continue through the existing `/api/paper` and `BookService` boundary. A flight action must never submit or mutate a paper order.

The UI should add **Lock & rehearse** to `DecisionCanvas`, a status strip to `DecisionLifecyclePage`, and a focused `DecisionFlightPage` with Evidence Ledger, Replay Cockpit, and Outcome Comparison. Review Hub should add flights needing reveal, matured outcomes, and reflection due. Trust Control Room should expose packet hash, cutoff coverage, fingerprints, provider status, assumptions, worker heartbeat, and reconciliation. Preserve the five-area navigation and index flight ID, symbol, thesis, claims, and review state in search. Every cockpit number must resolve to a packet field or deterministic formula.

| Build window | Deliverable and exit condition |
|---|---|
| **Days 0–15: contract and local slice** | Define `DecisionFlight`, statuses, canonical JSON hashing, eligibility/unresolved states, and event names. Link a complete local plan to a packet, import it into `flight-sim`, support one symbol/daily bars/blind action/no-trade, save and reload the result, and prove the main and practice book hashes are unchanged. |
| **Days 16–35: server authority** | Add prepare/get/action/step/finalize/export routes, cursor/version checks, idempotent action IDs, manifest fingerprints, and explicit unavailable responses. Fault tests must cover duplicate actions, changed packet hashes, stale cursors, malformed history, missing news, same-bar ambiguity, provider faults, and attempted paper-book mutation. |
| **Days 36–55: paper and review join** | Link an explicit existing paper order without conflating it with replay, add live-outcome maturity using completed exchange bars and the current calendar, and render the three timelines. Review after browser restart must still open the exact packet and linked order. |
| **Days 56–75: trust and learning** | Register packet, replay, and outcome claims in `EvidenceClaim`; render “Why am I seeing this?” for derived values; add coverage/missingness panels, consented reflection prompts, and exports. Keep raw journal data local by default. |
| **Days 76–90: pilot and readiness** | Freeze the product measurement protocol, run no-lookahead and paper-isolation suites, perform restart/restore and simulated-clock maturity drills, and run a bounded internal pilot on real provider data only where coverage is available. Decide whether the feature is ready for a separate InsForge schema review; do not enable hosted cutover as part of this feature. |

This sequencing is practical because the current repository already uses a local single-profile store, and the migration record says local data remains authoritative until fresh hosted isolation and readback checks pass ([`INSFORGE_MIGRATION.md`](../INSFORGE_MIGRATION.md)). Hosted `decision_flights` tables can come later if query volume or row-level access justifies them. The first WOW must not be held hostage by deployment work, and adding InsForge now would create a second migration surface rather than user value.

## The moat compounds through trustworthy decision history

The moat flywheel starts with a useful structured decision: source-linked thesis, counter-thesis, risk budget, and no-trade option. The locked packet makes review fair because later knowledge cannot rewrite the starting point. Replay adds standardized outcomes and counterfactuals; post-event review separates thesis, execution, process, and outcome variance. That creates a reusable playbook of setups, evidence patterns, timing errors, and risk-boundary changes. More reviewed flights make the next flight faster and more relevant.

The compounding asset is not raw price data, generated opinions, or a secret user score. It is a permissioned map of **intent → evidence → plan → action → simulated execution → market path → reflection**. Quant-Plus can describe process without forecasting: decisions are more complete when invalidation is recorded, results deteriorate after widened stops, or action often follows stale evidence. Those are descriptive learning outputs. A broker can import fills; rebuilding source-linked history, corrections, rejected ideas, and process records is harder.

The flywheel should remain privacy-preserving. Keep private workspace data—holdings, journals, identifiable behavior—separate from de-identified evaluation records and any later aggregate insight. Do not train across users by default. Give users export, deletion, retention, and training-use controls. India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 makes purpose, notice, correction/erasure, grievance, and security design constraints rather than afterthoughts ([India Code: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/19537), 2023). The moat becomes stronger when users trust it with their reasoning, not when the product extracts more behavior than it can explain.

The proof gates should be staged. First, engineering integrity: **100% of locked packets preserve plan fields, availability times, trust snapshot, replay manifest, and source hashes; zero replay actions change book version, cash, holdings, orders, fills, or ledger; no future bars/news appear before the cursor; and every unresolved case has a reason.** Second, workflow usefulness: internal targets of 60% eligible-plan-to-flight starts, 50% completion, 50% matured reflection, and a sub-three-minute lock flow after plan completion. Third, learning: measure answers to what users knew, believed, risked, and learned; hindsight edits, invalidation capture, no-trade completion, playbook reuse, and risk-limit adherence. Paper P&L is not the primary metric.

Only after the workflow is stable should Quant-Plus make stronger forecast claims. Any probability needs a preregistered target, point-in-time data, baselines, temporal holdouts, cost treatment, calibration, coverage, abstention, and matured outcomes. Guo et al. show why confidence is not automatically probability ([On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html), 2017); Bailey et al. show why repeated strategy selection creates false backtest confidence ([The Probability of Backtest Overfitting](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253), 2014). The repository’s 500 matured forecasts per claimed horizon and 200 human-reviewed copilot answers are prudent internal gates, not legal standards.

“Undefeatable” is not a guarantee. The product becomes difficult to displace only if four kinds of evidence accumulate together: users repeatedly finish and review flights; the packet and replay remain trustworthy under faults and provider gaps; the personal playbook saves real cognitive work without increasing harmful activity; and the corpus of point-in-time Indian decisions becomes useful for evaluation and process coaching. A competitor can copy the name, the replay slider, or the chat prompt. It is harder to copy years of permissioned, high-quality decision lineage, correction history, event snapshots, review labels, and user-specific workflow memory—especially if Quant-Plus remains honest about what it does not know.

## Guardrails keep the WOW from becoming a liability

The initial capability ladder should stop at evidence, scenarios, and paper practice. The model may organize evidence, ask clarifying questions, draft a counter-thesis, identify contradictions, and explain a replay. It must not issue a personalized buy/sell/hold command from a user’s holdings, income, age, or inferred vulnerability; it must not place, amend, or cancel an order; and it must not silently change a thesis, size, stop, or replay selection. The user makes the paper decision, deterministic code enforces supported limits, and the audit stream records the difference between model text, user action, policy result, and paper outcome. “No trade,” “insufficient evidence,” and “unresolved” must be first-class outcomes.

This boundary is important in India. SEBI maintains separate Investment Adviser and Research Analyst frameworks, including updated Research Analyst guidelines dated January 8, 2025 ([SEBI Research Analysts Regulations](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/regulations/jul-2023/securities-and-exchange-board-of-india-research-analysts-regulations-2014-last-amended-on-july-4-2023-_74008.html), 4 Jul 2023, and [SEBI Guidelines for Research Analysts](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jan-2025/guidelines-for-research-analysts_90634.html), 8 Jan 2025). SEBI’s June 6, 2022 warning addressed unregulated algorithmic platforms making huge-return and future-performance claims ([SEBI caution to investors](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2022/1654859706888.pdf), 6 Jun 2022). Its February 4, 2025 circular on safer retail algorithmic trading places brokers and exchanges in the risk-management chain ([SEBI safer participation circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/feb-2025/safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading_91614.html), 4 Feb 2025). These sources do not classify this exact feature; counsel must review the final workflow, audience, compensation, security-specificity, personalization, and transaction effects before launch.

The product should follow the spirit of NIST’s Govern–Map–Measure–Manage approach: maintain a model inventory, data charter, incident process, prompt-injection tests, provenance checks, privacy boundaries, abstention, and monitoring ([NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), 26 Jan 2023; [NIST Generative AI Profile](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1), 26 Jul 2024). Failure modes are concrete. **Hindsight leakage** requires packet hashes, availability cutoffs, fingerprints, and future-hidden replay. **False precision** requires unresolved daily-bar cases, visible spread/latency/participation assumptions, and no exchange-realistic language. **Coverage gaps** must produce an unavailable flight with a reason, never synthetic data. **Over-trading** requires no urgency prompts or win leaderboard, plus cooling-off and loss-chasing visibility; Barber and Odean link excessive trading with worse outcomes ([Trading Is Hazardous to Your Wealth](https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00226), 2000). **Privacy overreach** requires local-first storage, consent, export/delete, and no default cross-user training. **Integration failure** is contained by separating replay from the paper ledger; **low repeat use** is addressed by progressive capture.

Generic AI signals, generic agents, options-first expansion, and charting breadth should remain supporting inputs or future gates rather than the flagship. Signals are weak without calibrated outcomes and are quickly copied. Agents are increasingly shipped by broker and platform incumbents. Options require observed chains, verified lot and expiry rules, historical Greeks, corporate-action treatment, and a reliable fill model that the current capability gate explicitly does not have ([`CapabilitiesPage.tsx`](../src/components/app/pages/CapabilitiesPage.tsx)). Charting breadth would put Quant-Plus directly against TradingView and Indian specialists with a weaker distribution and mobile position. Decision Flight Recorder uses the assets Quant-Plus already has and gives those assets a product-shaped reason to exist.

## Conclusion

The strategic move is to make Quant-Plus remember a decision better than any generic terminal: freeze the evidence, expose the assumptions, rehearse the thesis blind, preserve the paper-account audit, and return with a process-level explanation. Build the first version on the current local runtime, current NSE cash-equity scope, current paper authority, current replay engine, and current review machinery; add breadth only after the flight proves that users repeatedly complete and learn from the loop. The right evidence of a moat will be a growing history of trustworthy, reviewed decisions—not a claim that the model predicts prices.

> **Positioning:** Quant-Plus is the paper-first Decision Flight Recorder for Indian equities—showing what was knowable, what was believed, what was risked, and what the decision teaches next.
