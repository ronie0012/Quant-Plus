## Still open — do not mark the roadmap complete

1. User-session confirmation, refreshed live two-user isolation/worker checks, and reviewed hosted cutover. The verified identity transfer itself has passed. Historical isolation reports are not fresh evidence. The old live harness changes project-wide email verification and must not be run casually during real-account onboarding.
2. Full field/action inventory and closure mapping; universal observation/availability lineage and point-in-time instrument/membership/corporate-action history; verified future/special calendars; measured provider/socket capacity and reconnection behavior.
3. Corporate-action accounting/adjusted history, a verified broker fee profile, complete execution latency assumptions, and live browser-closed/restart/halt evidence. The implemented settlement model is not full clearing integration.
4. Remaining ingestion/backfill/entitlement coverage, correction/retraction propagation, multilingual entity interpretation and reviewed extraction quality. PDF parsing does not supply OCR for scanned documents.
5. A genuinely shared immutable cross-stage decision bundle, fair scheduling/cancellation across legacy jobs, forecast contracts/registry/outcome coverage, ablations and registered temporal baseline comparisons. Pre-synthesis rejection alone does not close Phase 7.
6. Challenger sentiment models, reviewed/adjudicated labels, frozen holdouts, drift/calibration/event studies and sufficient matured outcomes. Do not substitute generated labels or simulated future returns.
7. Remaining product work: configurable dashboard panels, chart annotations/event overlays/zoom, expanded screener factors, portfolio correlation/net attribution, volume/news/event alerts and cooldown policy, archived-evidence replay/manifests, and a validated observed Options workflow.
8. At least 1,000 reviewed news items, 200 reviewed copilot answers, and the specified matured forecast evidence. The numeric checker is conservative engineering protection, not a measured 95% factual-precision result or a proof that every qualitative claim is correct.
9. Ten consecutive real trading-session soaks, broader performance/fault/manual accessibility/touch evidence, independent review and release claims reconciled to these results. No release gate is self-certified here.

No completion percentage or error-free guarantee is claimed.
